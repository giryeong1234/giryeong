01 · Profile

I am a second-year Bachelor of Nutrition Science student in the School of Clinical Sciences at Monash University. Awarded a Department of Nutrition, Dietetics and Food Summer Vacation Research Scholarship, I am currently investigating sleeping and eating patterns among shift workers participating in a weight-loss intervention.

With frontline and management experience across hospitality in Australia, South Korea, and Cambodia — plus bilingual Korean–English interpreting for KOTRA — I bring operational discipline, financial stewardship, and cross-cultural communication to every team I join.
